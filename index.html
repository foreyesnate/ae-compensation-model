02How commission scales

Quota is measured in new monthly recurring revenue you sign. Commission is paid as a multiple of that monthly figure — sign a client at $349 a month at a 2.5× rate and you earn $873 once, not every month. The multiple rises in bands as you climb past quota, and reaching a higher band never reduces the rate on the revenue below it.

How a month is paid

Commission is calculated on each month's signings on their own, so a strong month is never averaged away by a slow one.

Setup and cleanup fees are paid at a flat rate and sit outside quota entirely. They never affect your attainment, which means a client who needs eighteen months of cleanup counts the same toward quota as one who needs none — there is no reason to hold a deal back.

Because cleanup pricing grows every month a prospect waits, signing sooner is cheaper for them. That is a real reason for a client to move, and it is yours to use.

Why we are showing you this

You should be able to see the economics you are working inside, not just your own line of them. It is the same arithmetic we use on our side.

Year one collects only part of the recurring revenue, because a client signed in October barely bills before December. The following year collects the full amount from everyone still on the books, with no commission attached — that has already been paid. Which is why a client who stays is worth so much more than one who does not.

Recurring revenue assumes billing begins in the signing month and that no client cancels during the year. Cleanup fees are calculated as the monthly rate multiplied by the months of cleanup, divided by two. The one-time rate applies to setup and cleanup fees combined.
